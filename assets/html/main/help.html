Help Center

Find answers to common questions

Getting Started

How do I create a new classroom?

Go to the Classroom section and click the "Create New Classroom" button. Fill in the required information and click "Create".

How can I reset my password?

Visit the Settings page and click on "Change Password" under Security settings. Follow the instructions to set a new password.

How do I invite students to my class?

In the Classroom section, click on your class and use the "Invite Students" button to share the class code or send email invitations.

Account Management

How do I update my profile information?

Go to the Profile section and click the "Edit Profile" button. Make your changes and click "Save".

How can I enable two-factor authentication?

In the Settings page, go to Security settings and toggle the "Two-Factor Authentication" switch.

Classroom Features

How do I create assignments?

In your classroom, click on the "Assignments" tab and use the "Create Assignment" button to set up a new task.

How can I track student progress?

Use the "Analytics" tab in your classroom to view detailed reports on student performance and engagement.
